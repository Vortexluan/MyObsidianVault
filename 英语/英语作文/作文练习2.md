1.一般来说，我们用from my perspective, in my opinion, in my view这样的表达 
2.只说high pressure似乎有点简单无聊了.we college students are certainly under heightened pressure...
3.under pressure的语境下，通常说operate，function等动词来说明在高压下人如机器一般运作。
4.certianly->undoubtedly... should->ought to
5.bail out其实是一个相对口语化的表达，不适合用在书面上，我们有一个词extricate表示解救.extricate ... from是常用的搭配。
implement avenue也是一个不常用的表达，这就像中文里说“实施途径”一样，我们通常说“提供途径”或者“探索途径”，不会说这种生硬的表达。
explore/pursue/provide avenues
implement measures/methods/strategies

but that does not mean we don't have alternative avenues to extricate us from this predicament.
