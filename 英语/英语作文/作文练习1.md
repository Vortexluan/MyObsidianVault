The picture depicts a scene in which dragon boats are sprinting on the river and valiantly trying to win, two elders are saying that the boat competition in their village gets thriving recently, faraway, audiences are watching and cheering for the competition on a bridge.

Not only should we keep our traditions and festivals intact, but we also need to come up with some innovative ways to awake potentiality which is still in hibernation of our culture. 修改：“The picture depicts a scene in which dragon boats are sprinting on the river and valiantly trying to win, two elders are saying that the boat competition in their village gets thriving recently, faraway, audiences are watching and cheering for the competition on a bridge.”

1.sprint通常更加用于描述人，我们可以考虑race这个单词，既适用于人也适用于交通工具
2.saying有点过于口语化了，可以考虑commenting，如果想要表达老人的惊叹的化可以写exclaiming with delight
3.上面提到delight，其实关于“高兴”“欣喜”是有很多表达方法的，ecastic, elated, exhilarated, jubilant, euphoric, rapturous, elysium...
4."sprinting on the river and valiantly trying to win"想要描述那种奋勇争先的感觉，valiantly其实并不合适，rigorously可能更适合描述那种生机勃勃万物竞发的场景（
5.之前已经知道但是没有想起来spectators这个词，相比于audience更贴切地描述看体育比赛的观众
6.如果要追求生僻，The picture depicts可以改为The illustration depicts/portrays...
7.要说的话araway, audiences are watching...也有点不正式，改成in the distance比较书面化一点
8.trying to win 也有点空泛,straining恰好能体现出那种尽全力的感觉,win直接换成victory算了。
事实上 their crew striving for victory, their crew exerting themselves for victory都是可以参考的

修改之后：The illustration vividly portrays a dynamic scene: dragon boats racing down the river, their crews rigorously straining for victory; two elders nearby exclaim that the boat competition in their village has lately thrived, while in the distance spectators cheer and applaud on a bridge.




”The picture showcases that the culture vestige of China is gradually attracting more attention from modern city to surberb village.“

1.culture vestige是基本上不用的搭配，我们一般说cultural heritage
cultural resources, cultural assets, intangible cultural heritage...
2.surberb是打错的，应是suburb.但是这个词通常用来表示郊区，是城市周边的区域，和乡下完全无关。rural, rustic, bucolic...都可以用来表示乡下的那种感觉，rustic和bucolic更加褒义性，乡村风光的那种感觉。可以考虑rural village/hamlet
3.attracting more attention...可以改进。is gradually garnering widespread attention/is gradually captivating world-wide interest...

修改之后:The picture showcases that the resurgence of cultural heritage in China is gradually garnering widespread interests from modern urban regions.



"A real cultural tradition should bind people together and guide them though difficulties through uniting with others. "

1.有没有注意到在写这篇作文的时候people这个单词用的太多了，我们还可以考虑individual和citizen，注意这个citizen表示公民，不是说居住在城市里面的人，和city这个词的词义不大有关系。
2.real未免有点口语化了，可以用genuine/veritable/authentic...
3.difficulties似乎也显得有点口语化，adversity/predicament也可以考虑
4.除了unite, bind, combine,我们还有 knit, forge, cement, weld这样表示把人们连接起来的词
5.collective solidarity可以直接理解为“集体团结”或者“集体连带”

修改之后：A veritable cultural tradition ought to knit citizens together and shepherd them through predicaments by forging collective solidarity.




"The function of bringing people together is such priceless in our modern time, in which people get seperated or even atomized by technology and urbanization.  "
1.seperate错，应是separate

修改之后：In this modern era where technology and urbanization separate and atomize individuals, the function of knitting people together is so invaluable.


"In my perspective, cultural tradition is a efficient measure which naturally could be implemented to rouse coherence within people. "
1.coherence表示连贯性，这里应该是cohesion凝聚力，凝聚性。
2.a-->an


“Not only should we keep our traditions and festivals intact, but we also need to come up with some innovative ways to awake potentiality which is still in hibernation of our culture. ”
1.表示手段，方式，方法除了measure，method，还有avenue
2.potential本身就可以表示名词
3.hibernation特指冬眠,dormant/dormancy表示休眠，用来表示潜藏的潜力，天赋，才能等,dormant potential是常用的用法

修改之后:Not only must we preserve our traditions and festivals intact, but we also need to implement innovative avenues to arouse dormant potential within our culture.


"From combing cultural icon with cut-edged technology to transforming old story into films, We can expect a flouring future waiting for us and our culture."
1.cut-edged应为cutting-edge
2.old story口语化了，考虑tradition lore/ancient narratives...

修改之后:From merging cultural icon with cutting-edge technology to transforming tradition lore to films, we could expect a flourishing and thriving future waiting for us and our culture.
